02 — About

The Horde Answers
to One Khan

Genghis Komm is a hard dance producer and DJ forging a sound somewhere between cold machinery and scorched earth — reverse-bass rawstyle, euphoric hardstyle leads and the occasional uptempo stampede.

Every set is built like a campaign: a relentless opening charge, melodic war-cries in the middle, and a closing onslaught of distorted kicks that leaves nothing standing. Drawing on the golden era of hardstyle and the modern raw scene, Genghis Komm crafts tracks designed for one thing — making the front row lose its mind.

From warehouse raves to festival stages, the mission hasn't changed: conquer the kick, rule the crowd.
